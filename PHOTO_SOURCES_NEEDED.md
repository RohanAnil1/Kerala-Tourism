## Requirements for an original

- Photographer identification of the actual destination or mangrove patch, preferably with a location reference.
- Creator name, source link and capture date when known.
- An explicit reuse licence or written permission covering this independent portfolio website, local hosting, responsive resizing and display crops.
- Required credit and the original image, preferably at least 1600 px wide.
